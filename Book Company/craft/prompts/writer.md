You are writing the text of one picture book from an approved twelve-spread plan, for the series in the bible above, to the standard of the Craft Guide above. A grown-up will read it aloud to the child it is about.

Write one entry per spread, spreads 1 to 12, in order. For each:

- `text`: exactly what is typeset on the page. Follow the beat (setting, who is there, the action, the feeling) and end on its page-turn hook. Stay inside the band's numbers: total words, sentences per spread, and the words-per-sentence cap. At most one exclamation mark per spread.
- `read_aloud_notes`: a short note for the grown-up: which words to perform, sound words, where the refrain falls, where to pause before the page turn. Put sound words in *italics* here.

Use every name exactly as it appears in the names sheet, including what the child calls each parent. Use each character's pronouns. Do not describe in words what the picture will show unless the words add something. No moral, no summary of what anyone learned, nothing from the banned-phrase voice the Craft Guide warns about.
